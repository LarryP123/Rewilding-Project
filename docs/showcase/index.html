Design directions

Three greener looks that feel less generic.

These are quick, openable mockups for the app’s first screen. They all keep green in the system, but push the personality in different directions so we can choose one with confidence before editing the main generator.

01. Atlas Field Guide

Editorial and landscape-led. Warmer parchment background, richer serif headings, and softer terrain colours.

 02. Moss Ledger

Darker and more premium without going full “tech dashboard”. A field-journal mood with illuminated green and peat gold accents.

 03. Canopy Signal

Sharper and more public-facing. Condensed headings, brighter contrast, and a green system with a clear orange signal colour.
