Using The Explorer

The explorer compares the same national hex grid under five policy scenarios. Each scenario uses a different percentile field from the scoring model, so the map shows how priorities change the national pattern.

Lighter cells are stronger under the active scenario. Darker cells are weaker. Stable core highlights places that remain strong across all scenarios. Contested highlights places that strengthen under some scenarios but not others.

Selecting a hex shows its active percentile, strongest scenario, best rank, and rank spread. Rank spread is an indicator of how sensitive that place is to policy trade-offs.
