Using The Explorer

The explorer compares the same national hex grid under five policy scenarios. Each scenario uses a different percentile field from the scoring model, so the map shows how priorities shift the shortlist.

Lighter cells are stronger under the active scenario. Darker cells are weaker. Stable core highlights places that remain strong across every scenario. Contested highlights places that only rise under some policy lenses.

Clicking a hex shows its active percentile, strongest scenario, best rank, and rank spread. Rank spread is a quick indicator of how sensitive that place is to policy tradeoffs.
